# CFS5 actual parapets and finite track connections

The former fixture drew isolated parapet studs and generic roof receivers. It
also placed some stud/track fasteners beyond the actual cut track end. This
change uses the actual upper wall studs, finite tracks and roof joists.

* 340 existing exterior studs continue to the source 600T125-43 cap. The source
  600S162-43 parapet selection, 36-inch roof-to-cap height, 54 mil roof clip
  and four No.10 screws per attached leg remain explicit.
* The existing fictional opening set-out leaves 70 header-supported shorter
  backspans and some 24/28-inch transition gaps. These remain recorded as A
  decisions requiring verification; the source full-storey/16-inch capacity
  is not assigned to them.
* Actual roof joists and cap tracks have local cutbacks and roll. Roof-sheet
  and track-web clearances name only the continuous studs they clear.
* Offset roof attachments, end-track clips and cap corner links are formed
  components with screws through the actual sheets. Their local geometry and
  added fasteners are A proposals, with no new capacity or fabrication claim.
* The two parallel roof edge joists move inboard to clear the through studs.
  Separate clips connect their actual webs to intact track webs, avoiding the
  stud clearance slots. Source spans and selected sections are retained.

Validation before native acceptance: 2,973 detail variants and 31,282 located
occurrences pass receiver placement checks. Nineteen targeted geometry tests
and 50 related regression tests pass. Twenty-one previews were visually
inspected; parapet bend labels were moved to separate leader rails and the
two overlapping examples were rechecked. Native completeness, complete IFC
return and final PDF acceptance are tracked separately by the 61-case harness.

The gold design records remain unchanged. S means a source design parameter;
A means an accepted fictional fit or layout. These drawings are design-test
fixtures, not a new engineering design or construction issue.
