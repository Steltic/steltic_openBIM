# CFS10 and CFS18 roof-header physical review

The former header recipe showed a generic HSS and a header one half-depth below
the connection workpoint. It omitted the selected cap tracks and ignored the
adjacent SBMF channel webs/flanges. The revised details extract all four actual
members, preserve their finite ends and carry their geometry into variant keys.

The source header and cap sections are retained. The cap is shown horizontally,
with its actual full web width and two A No.12 screws per maximum12in station.
The delegated bottom truss chord is raised to clear the cap web; top-chord and
roof-panel datums stay unchanged. Exact cap orientation, overhang, attachment,
heel geometry, header cutbacks, tab projection and bolt stations are accepted
fictional fit inputs, not verified supplier design.

The source HSS-face shear tab occupies the SBMF web. Explicit A opposed
3/4in-long x6-1/8in-high web reliefs resolve that conflict; CFS10's bearing
plates have matching open-top notches. Drawings and the change register state
that the original uncut SBMF capacity does not verify this modification. No
analysis or capacity checks are rerun. CFS10 retains its specified3/8in tab,
two5/8in A325-N bolts and paired1/4in x6in welds. CFS18 lacks that source
connection selection, so those same local parts are A rather than S.

Validation: 8 header/contact/datum tests and 6 existing wing tests pass. All
header, cap and moment-joint variants in the two development fit audits match
their actual model receivers. Thirteen selected PDF preview sheets were
visually inspected: CFS10 C001-C008 and CFS18 C001-C005. Full native acceptance
of these two buildings remains pending other framing/connection corrections.
