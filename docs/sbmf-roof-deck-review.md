# CFS18 roof deck physical review

The roof-deck boundary previously sat on the truss workline. It now follows the actual inclined top-flange bearing plane, with explicit thin folded sheets, joggled sidelaps, and located fasteners. Each sheet is one folded product; profile strips describe its geometry, not separately fabricated plates.

The saved selection provides the 1.5 in. 22 ga wide-rib deck, No. 12 support screws in a 36/4 pattern, No. 10 sidelap screws at 12 in., and required manufacturer diaphragm strength. Rib flats, 0.0295 in. thickness, 0.75 in. joggled overlap, flange gauge, partial sheets, edge distances, and a folded ridge closure are adopted example geometry. Continuous 200 ft site-formed runs and the altered fit require manufacturer verification; saved calculations do not certify these proposals.

Seam screws pass through both actual sheet plies and the finite truss flange. Sidelap and ridge screws pass through their actual receiving sheets. Drawings use a plan, thin-sheet section, and outlined isometric to show the assembly.

Validation: 17 deck/bearing/collector/mezzanine/roof-seat tests pass, including all inclined fastener rays, sheet cover and screw spacing, finite truss receivers, and sheet thickness. Eleven rendered sheets were inspected across both slopes, full/partial sidelaps, and the ridge. Native replay v146 has four unresolved mezzanine floor/diaphragm source mappings; the deck mapping and joint/component/receiver checks pass. This change does not claim whole-building completion.
