# CFS10 and CFS18 wing datums and wall straps

The CFS10 office roof previously reused the floor finish thickness, putting
the roof joists through the top-track web. The two wings now use the exact
source finish thicknesses below the top-of-finish datum: 19/32-inch roof WSP,
and 23/32-inch floor WSP plus 1-inch (CFS10) or 1.5-inch (CFS18) gypsum.
Joist tops, panel undersides and bearing track webs share the same datum.
The final east-edge joist is included even where the last spacing is shorter
than the nominal spacing. Access-door cripple-stud connection points follow
the actual raised stud feet.

Ground tracks in the main hall belong to display level 3. They now use their
actual ground elevation to select the slab anchorage detail instead of an
invented intermediate-floor stack.

Strap-end details use the actual bay chord packs, finite cut ends and separate
planes for the two continuous diagonals. Source sections and end screw counts
are retained. Collector plates, additional per-ply receiving screws, packing
and local screw stations are A proposals. The original direct strap/chord
capacity is explicitly not assigned to an added collector plate.

These corrections remove the wing/track intersections and the generic strap
receivers. Remaining main-roof, wall-slip, secondary-framing and catwalk
interfaces are separate ongoing checks; this change does not claim complete
native acceptance for either building. The gold design records are unchanged.
