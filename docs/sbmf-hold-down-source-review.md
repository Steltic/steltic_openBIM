# SBMF source mapping and hold-down fit

CFS10 and CFS18 now map every saved analysis element exactly once to its actual
physical frame column or paired beam. Source node coordinates verify the mapping,
including the explicit span/station axis relabelling. Beam extensions and column
heads retain their physical geometry and reference their own located attachments.
The original saved design evidence is unchanged.

Hold-down details now extract the actual chord and rod, rather than drawing
substitute sections at the origin. The rod has an adopted 1.5in offset into one
channel cavity, clear of the paired chord webs. Four through-web bolts contact
both webs, a solid pack and the bent bracket. The main-wall source cast anchor is
a short base anchor; wing story rods retain their actual floor couplers, which
move with the rods. Source chord sections and anchor/story rod diameters remain.

Source main-wall bracket thickness and bolt sizes are retained. Bracket outlines,
bolt pitch/gauge, pack, rod offset, supplier take-up envelope, 12in embedment and
embedded plate are explicitly adopted fictional fit inputs. Wing bracket hardware
is adopted. These choices do not establish local chord, bracket or foundation
capacity and do not replace delegated engineering requirements.

Validation: six focused tests check immutable source evidence, all 468 analysis
element mappings, attachment ownership, all 80 hold-down bolt groups, rod/chord
clearance and all 32 story couplers. The development fit audit reports no CFS18
receiver failures; CFS10's unrelated remaining families are still open. Ten PDF
preview sheets (five per building) were visually checked. Native completeness,
IFC verification and the final drawing package remain separate required checks.
