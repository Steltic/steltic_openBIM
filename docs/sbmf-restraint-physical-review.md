# SBMF flange restraint receivers

CFS10 and CFS18 previously showed a generic truss vertical in each flange-strut
detail without locating that vertical in the physical building. Struts now sit
below their actual SBMF bottom flanges. Each has a real 54mil receiving vertical
and a separate cap-shoe attachment to a modeled delegated truss bottom chord.

CFS10 retains its source 24in 362S162-54 struts, two No.12 flange screws and
one 1/2in vertically slotted receiving bolt. Near-column stations are measured
18in apart from the column face, with no interval exceeding 8ft elsewhere.
The exact flange stations, inward perimeter orientation, added trusses,
vertical extensions, cap shoes, cap welds/bolts and slot travel are A fictional
inputs. CFS18's 1.39kip restraint requirement remains attached; its local strut
size and connections are A. No supplier strength or stiffness is certified.

Existing 4ft truss locations remain. Extra receiving trusses lie within the
roof footprint and reduce local spacing. They use the same delegated roof
geometry and actual header/cap bearing details as the other roof trusses.
Saved source design records remain unchanged.

Validation: 20 tests pass across restraint, roof-seat and header suites. These
check all 676 struts and receiving extensions, actual contact of every fastener
with its connected solids, cap-bolt clearance from chord ends, source length,
restraint spacing, retained roof grids and unchanged gold records. Development
receiver-fit audits have no remaining restraint-family failures. Four selected
PDF sheets per building were visually checked, including both directions,
perimeter fit and the separate receiving cap. Other building corrections and
full native/IFC acceptance are still pending.
