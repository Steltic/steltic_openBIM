# CFS18 mezzanine floor zone

The previous model combined plywood and gypsum in one layer and omitted the source rim, squash blocks, bearing plates, stiffeners and floor-transfer fasteners. The floor now has separate 23/32 in. WSP and 1.5 in. gypsum, with actual wall bearing and material cut-outs at the unchanged 120 in. finish level.

The source 10.25 in. paired 600S162-54 squash blocks and 3/4 x 3.5 x 6 in. A36 plates establish a lower collector bearing plane of 107.5 in. The source 1000T200-68 catalog rim has a 10.2051 in. outside depth, so its 0.07615 in. lower packing is an explicit adopted fit. Rim flanges are cut clear at chord blocks while the full web remains continuous. Packing is physically split around the blocks. Plate rod-edge notches and two formed retention clips are adopted; the clips are not claimed as checked uplift or shear-transfer connections.

The 1000S250-97 joists retain their selected section and maximum 16 in. spacing. Local paired stations and shifted edge stations clear the six-inch-deep chord blocks. Matching bearing studs follow those joists. Adjacent spans lap with touching webs over the two interior walls. Each of 228 joist ends has its source 600S162-43 x 9.62 in. stiffener and three No. 10 web screws. Small top/end packs, lap retention and exact set-out are adopted rather than silently altering source lengths.

Each of eight 10 ft strap panels retains five 5/8 in. A325-N bolts at 24 in. through the upper track, WSP and rim top flange. Sixteen No. 12 screws per panel join the lower rim flange to the actual 97 mil track through the packing and outer collector ply. The screw grip ends at the receiving web underside, not the bottom of the deep track leg. Field fixing retains the source maximum six-inch schedule outside the block interruptions. Source diaphragm sheathing attachments are a separate remaining work item.

All details extract the finite physical receivers. Gypsum holdbacks name only actual upper-wall members that intersect those strips; ordinary openings remain checked. Tests cover every declared fastener receiver, source lengths, full retained rim webs, lap fit, maximum joist spacing, source bolt/screw counts, separate layers and finite member-clearance declarations. No source calculation is changed, and no capacity is assigned to the adopted geometry without verification.

Validation: 22 focused regression tests passed; the five floor tests were repeated after correcting the screw grip. Twenty-five rendered preview sheets were inspected, with the rim fixing views rechecked after that correction. Native replay clears the floor source mappings and physical/schema checks; the two mezzanine diaphragm mappings remain. Whole-building completion and final IFC/drawing delivery are not claimed by this change.
