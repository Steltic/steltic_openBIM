# SBMF wall connection fit

CFS10 storefront girts and CFS18 side/end-wall girts now have their actual
outward bypass locations, finite end cuts, receiving columns/posts and folded
clips. Boxed storefront girts use through bolts and solid packing between both
webs. Added clip dimensions, bolt access arrangements and local capacities are
explicit A fit decisions. CFS18's unselected intermediate field studs are
removed; its source braced-bay studs and intermediate girt wall system remain.
Cladding offsets and source sag-angle restraint remain separate coordination
requirements, rather than an inferred capacity approval of the clip.

All six CFS18 end-wall wind posts now fit directly between the real end-frame
channel webs. Their adopted four flange bolts have vertical slots; source
24ft post height and the no-gravity-support requirement remain unchanged.

CFS18's source 11.25ft braced-bay chord worklines replace the earlier generic
3in chord insets. This lets the source L3x3x3/8 x6in tier restraint angles meet
the real column and chord, retaining the source 1.5in reaction lever arm and
paired 1/4in x6in welds. The tier tracks are two continuous pieces with touching
webs and No.12 seam screws at maximum 12in. Actual end cuts clear the chord;
adopted internal chord-web clips support the track ends. Slot/keeper details
and clip capacities remain A. Upper and lower strap collectors leave the
central tier slot clear instead of overlapping it.

Validation covers every girt and wind-post fastener contact, both track webs,
source angle weld lengths, source seam spacing and tier-slot clearance from
strap collector plates. The full CFS18 development receiver-fit audit has no
remaining failures. Fourteen girt and four wall-connection PDF preview sheets
were visually checked. This is geometric/source validation; native acceptance
and IFC comparison remain separate required steps.
